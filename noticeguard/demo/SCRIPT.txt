NOTICEGUARD — MAYA DEMO, 90 SECONDS
===================================

SETUP (before recording)
- Two tabs, hard-refresh both (Ctrl+Shift+R), let them load:
  A: https://noticeguard.ruleandrecord.com/?demo=maya
  B: https://noticeguard.ruleandrecord.com/?demo=maya-v3
- In each tab click "Licence certificate" in the left list (PDF shows).
- Tab A: arrow to the finding on clause 4.1 (monetised video). Leave it.
- Tab B: arrow to the finding on clause 4.1 / 4.2 (Pro required). Leave it.
- Full screen, bookmarks bar hidden, demo dropdown closed.

SCRIPT
------
0-12s  Tab A. Show claim journey (left) and PDF certificate (middle).
SAY: Maya is a synthetic creator. She licensed a track for a
monetised video and still got a copyright claim. The licensor's
website today says her licence doesn't cover monetised use.
She's about to give up.

12-30s Point at highlighted 4.1 on the PDF. Click next arrow to the
       6.1 governing-terms finding. PDF scrolls to the marked clause.
SAY: NoticeGuard reads her actual certificate. Clause 4.1 permits
monetised video. Clause 6.1 says the terms on her purchase date
govern, so today's change doesn't take her rights away.
The AI only highlights. The rules decide.

30-45s Click "Next step". Hold on EVIDENCE READY and the lowest-risk route.
SAY: Her evidence is ready for a dispute. The first route is the
low-risk one: ask the licensor to have its administrator release
the claim. Nothing is filed for her.

45-55s Scroll to the draft, click one sentence. Chain opens to the quote.
SAY: The draft is built only from confirmed facts. Every sentence
clicks back to the clause behind it.

55-65s Switch to Tab B.
SAY: Now the what-if. Same creator, same track, but she bought in
July, after the terms changed.

65-82s Point at highlighted 4.1 and 4.2 on the v3 PDF. Click "Next step".
       Hold on EVIDENCE GAP and the "Not supported" component.
SAY: Her v3 Standard licence covers non-monetised video only.
Monetised use needs Pro. That part of her statement isn't
supported, so no draft is produced, and she is told exactly
what is missing.

82-90s Stay on the gap screen.
SAY: Same documents, different evidence, different answer.
NoticeGuard tells her where she stands. Maya decides.

RULES
- Never say "verified" or "you will win". Say "evidence ready",
  "supported", "confirmed by document".
- Do not open Leo, Add evidence, or the benchmark page.
- Blank PDF pane? Wait two seconds, it falls back automatically.
- Fumble? Reload the same link. Both demos run from cache.
