NOTICEGUARD — MAYA DEMO, 90 SECONDS (LIVE DRAG AND DROP)
========================================================

SETUP (before recording)
- File manager window open at noticeguard/demo/01-maya-original
  (6 files: claim notice, receipt, certificate PDF, terms, track page,
  video export). Place it beside the browser so you can drag across.
- Tab A: https://noticeguard.ruleandrecord.com  (home page, empty).
- Tab B: the what-if, prepared in advance. Drop the 6 files from
  02-maya-what-if, fill the same form, Analyse. Click "Licence
  certificate", arrow to the 4.1 / 4.2 finding. Leave it.
- Hard-refresh both tabs first (Ctrl+Shift+R). Full screen.
- Form values, both cases: Name "Maya Ortiz", Channel "Maya Draws",
  Monetised YES, Step DISPUTE. Address, phone, notes empty. Flags off.

SCRIPT
------
0-15s  Tab A. Select all 6 files in the file manager, drag them onto the
       drop zone. The form opens. Type name and channel, tick Monetised
       yes, Step dispute. Click Analyse. Result appears in ~1 second.
SAY: Maya is a synthetic creator. She licensed a track for a
monetised video and still got a copyright claim. Today the licensor
says her licence doesn't cover monetised use. She drops in
everything she has: the claim, the receipt, her certificate, the
terms, the track page, the video export.

15-32s Click "Licence certificate" on the left. Her PDF appears with
       clauses marked. Point at 4.1. Arrow to the 6.1 governing-terms
       finding; the PDF scrolls to it.
SAY: NoticeGuard reads her actual certificate. Clause 4.1 permits
monetised video. Clause 6.1 says the terms on her purchase date
govern, so today's change doesn't take her rights away.
The AI only highlights. The rules decide.

32-47s Click "Next step". Hold on EVIDENCE READY and the lowest-risk route.
SAY: Her evidence is ready for a dispute. First route, lowest risk:
ask the licensor to have its administrator release the claim.
Nothing is filed for her.

47-56s Scroll to the draft, click one sentence. Chain opens to the quote.
SAY: The draft uses only confirmed facts. Every sentence clicks back
to the clause behind it.

56-64s Switch to Tab B.
SAY: The what-if. Same creator, same track, same six documents, but
she bought in July, after the terms changed.

64-82s Point at highlighted 4.1 and 4.2 on the v3 PDF. Click "Next step".
       Hold on EVIDENCE GAP and the "Not supported" component.
SAY: Her v3 Standard licence covers non-monetised video only.
Monetised use needs Pro. That part of her statement isn't
supported, so no draft is produced, and she sees exactly what
is missing.

82-90s Stay on the gap screen.
SAY: Same documents, different evidence, different answer.
NoticeGuard tells her where she stands. Maya decides.

RULES
- Never say "verified" or "you will win". Say "evidence ready",
  "supported", "confirmed by document".
- Do not open Leo, Add evidence, or the benchmark page.
- Document types are guessed from the filenames; do not change them.
- If the drop misfires, click the drop zone and pick the 6 files.
- Blank PDF pane? Wait two seconds, it falls back automatically.
- Fumble? "New check", drop again. Analysis runs from cache, ~1 s.
