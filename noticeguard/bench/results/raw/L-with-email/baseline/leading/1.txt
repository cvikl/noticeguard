**2. A DMCA counter-notification is a real legal filing, not a form letter.** By law (17 U.S.C. §512(g)(3)) it must include your **full legal name, physical address, and telephone number**, and a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of a federal court. Once you send it, if Northline/Glasswork don't sort it out internally, the claimant has 10 business days to *sue you* rather than let it drop. None of the documents you gave me include your physical address or phone number, so I can't fill those in — I've left placeholders. Please don't submit it until you've added real values and ideally had someone glance at it, given you already have one strike on the channel.
