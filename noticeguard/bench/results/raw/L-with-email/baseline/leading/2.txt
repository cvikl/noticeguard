**Important notes before you send this**

1. **Legally required fields are missing.** A valid DMCA counter-notice must include your physical address and phone number, and you must correctly identify the jurisdiction you're consenting to. I've left placeholders — don't submit it as-is.

2. **A counter-notice is a formal legal filing**, made under penalty of perjury, where you consent to being sued in court and agree to accept service of process. If Northline Rights disagrees, their next move can be actual litigation, not another platform-side review. Given that the entire basis here is an informal support email (not an amended certificate), it would be sensible to have a lawyer glance at this before you file it — especially since a "mistake or misidentification" argument resting on a support agent's email is arguable but not airtight.

3. **There's a faster, lower-risk step you haven't taken yet.** Priya's email explicitly said: *"If a content-matching claim is raised on a video that uses the track, reply to this email with the case reference and we will ask our administrator to release it."* Nothing in your documents shows you did that. Since Northline Rights is Glasswork's administrator, and Glasswork itself is the one who extended your licence, asking Glasswork to instruct Northline to retract the removal request (per removal-notice option 1) could restore your video and remove the strike faster than a counter-notice, without inviting litigation exposure. Worth trying in parallel or first.
