**One thing worth flagging, not blocking:** The current published terms (v3, effective 1 June 2025) say Standard tier is non-commercial only and monetised use needs Pro — different from what the certificate says applied to Maya's March 2025 purchase (v2). This looks like Glasswork later tightened the Standard tier, but Maya's certificate explicitly locks in "Licence Terms version 2," which is the version that should govern a purchase made under it. This is a plausible/standard arrangement (terms-at-time-of-purchase apply), so it doesn't undermine the dispute — but if Northline Rights or Glasswork pushes back citing v3, you may need Glasswork's support to confirm in writing that Maya's certificate/order predates and is governed by v2. Also, "Maya Ortiz" (licensee) vs. "Maya Draws" (channel) isn't explicitly tied together in the documents, though this is minor and easily addressed by the channel owner's name matching.

Given the certificate is squarely on point, dated before the video, and explicitly invites use in disputes, this is enough to file the dispute confidently.
