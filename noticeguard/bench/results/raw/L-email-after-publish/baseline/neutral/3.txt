## Does the evidence support a counter‑notice?

**The core legal statement in a DMCA counter‑notice** is that you have a *good‑faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification*, plus consent to jurisdiction and a perjury declaration. Here's how your documents map to that:

**What's solid:**
- You purchased a genuine **Standard licence** for *Glasslight* on 19 Aug 2025 (receipt + certificate, order GA-2025-08231).
- The track page (07) and the licensor's own terms (06, §10.1) confirm that **Northline Rights — the claimant — administers claims for this track *on behalf of* Glasswork Audio**, the actual copyright owner. So Northline is Glasswork's agent, not an independent rights holder.
