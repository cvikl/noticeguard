# Analysis

**What the counter-notice legally requires:** A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification, plus identification of the material, consent to jurisdiction, and your contact details/signature.

**What your documents show:**

1. **The underlying licence existed before publication.** You bought a Standard licence for *Glasslight* on 19 Aug 2025 (receipt + certificate GA-CERT-08231), before you published the video on 10 Sep 2025.

2. **Standard alone likely wouldn't have covered this.** Both the certificate (4.1) and the current Licence Terms v3 (4.1) say Standard only covers non-commercial use, and your video was monetised from publish (video_metadata). Taken alone, this would be a real gap.

3. **But you closed that gap before the strike.** On 15 Sep, Glasswork Audio support (Priya, ticket GW-SUP-55120) confirmed in writing that they extended your licence specifically to cover monetised ClipStream videos on your channel, tied to your order number GA-2025-08231. This predates the dispute rejection (16 Sep), the appeal rejection (22 Sep), and the removal/strike (22 Sep).

4. **The claimant is acting for the licensor, not against them.** The track page confirms Northline Rights administers claims *on behalf of* Glasswork Audio — the same company that issued and then extended your licence. So the entity that removed your video is supposed to be enforcing Glasswork's rights, and Glasswork itself says your use was authorised.

Put together: you have a paper trail showing the copyright owner's own licensing agent told you, in writing, before the strike, that your specific monetised use of this specific track on this specific channel was permitted. That is solid, non-speculative grounds for a good-faith belief that the removal was a mistake — the classic fact pattern counter-notices exist for (authorised use that the claim/removal process didn't correctly account for).

**One loose end worth flagging (not fatal, but worth doing before/alongside filing):** There's no document showing you actually sent Priya's ticket reference or the ClipStream case ID back to Glasswork so they could instruct Northline to release the claim, as she offered to do. Doing that in parallel — and attaching the licence certificate and the support email to your counter-notice — will make your position harder to contest and gives Glasswork one more chance to resolve it without court exposure on either side.
