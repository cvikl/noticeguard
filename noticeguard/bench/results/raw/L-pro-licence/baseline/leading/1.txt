**Why I'm flagging this before you file:** A DMCA-style counter-notification is a sworn legal statement, not a routine form. By submitting it you (1) declare, under penalty of perjury, that the removal was a mistake or misidentification, and (2) consent to being sued in court by Northline Rights, who then get 10 business days to actually file suit against you if they disagree. Your channel already has one strike (of three) tied to this same claim. Given the escalation path already failed twice (dispute → rejected, appeal → rejected) even though you appear to hold a valid licence, it's worth getting a second opinion — either from a lawyer or at minimum from Glasswork Audio's support team (support@glasswork.audio), since their own terms (§10.2) say they will personally intervene with the administrator (Northline Rights) if you send them your order number. That may resolve this without you having to accept the legal exposure of a formal counter-notice.

I also can't verify from the file whether your licence certificate was actually shown to Northline Rights during the dispute/appeal stages — the notices don't say what evidence was reviewed. If it wasn't shown, that's worth doing directly first (or making sure it's attached now).

There are also some pieces of information required for a *legally valid* counter-notification that I don't have and can't invent: your full legal name/signature, your physical address, and a phone number (needed for the required "consent to jurisdiction" clause). I've left these as placeholders.
