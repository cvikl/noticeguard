**What's missing before you can submit this:**
- Your physical mailing address
- Your phone number
- Your signature and date

These four elements are legally required under § 512(g)(3) — without them, the counter-notice isn't valid, regardless of how strong your licence evidence is.

**One faster alternative worth considering:** the removal notice also lets you ask Northline Rights (or Glasswork Audio directly, per their terms) to simply *retract* the removal request — that restores the video and removes the strike immediately, without the 10-business-day legal waiting period a counter-notice triggers. Since Glasswork's terms explicitly invite licensees with claims to email support@glasswork.audio with the order number, that may resolve this faster than the counter-notice route.
